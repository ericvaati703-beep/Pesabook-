# PesaBook

## Main Purpose
Help small shopkeepers recover money faster by tracking customer debts and making it easy to remind customers to pay.

## Home Screen
The shopkeeper immediately sees:
- Total money owed
- A list of customers who owe money
- Warning icons for risky or overdue debts
- A + Add Debt button

## Add Debt
For a new customer: Name, Phone number (optional), Amount, Save

## Customer Page
When the shopkeeper taps a customer, they see:
- Name, Phone number, Current balance
- Buttons: Payment, Add New Debt, Send Reminder
- Transaction history below

## Send Reminder
Prepares a reminder message the shopkeeper can send via SMS or WhatsApp.

## Record Payment
Tap Record Payment, enter amount paid, save. The app automatically reduces the balance, updates history, updates Home screen.

## Fully Paid Customer
When balance reaches KES 0, the customer moves to Archive. If they take credit again, their record reactivates with history intact.

## Offline First
The app works without internet. Data is saved locally. Cloud syncing can be added later.

## Core Value
PesaBook is a debt tracking and follow-up system — not a payment app. It helps shopkeepers know who owes them, avoid forgetting debts, follow up consistently, and recover money faster.
